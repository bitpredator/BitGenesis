# Code of Conduct - BitGenesis

## Our Commitment

BitGenesis is committed to providing a respectful, structured and constructive environment for all contributors and users.

We aim to foster a collaborative space focused on engineering quality, clarity of thought, and long-term architectural integrity.

All participants are expected to act professionally and in good faith.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Respectful and constructive communication
- Focus on technical arguments rather than personal opinions
- Willingness to improve and iterate ideas
- Acceptance of architectural decisions after discussion
- Clear and precise reporting of issues or improvements

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Disrespectful or offensive comments
- Personal attacks or harassment of any kind
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or development workflow
- Submission of low-quality or intentionally misleading contributions
- Ignoring established project guidelines without justification

---

## Architectural Respect

BitGenesis is an architecture-driven project.

This means:

- Architectural decisions are made carefully and documented via ADRs.
- Disagreement is allowed, but must be supported by technical reasoning.
- Once a decision is accepted, it should be respected in implementation.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Code reviews
- Documentation discussions
- Any form of project-related communication

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to:

- Request clarification or modification of contributions
- Reject contributions that violate guidelines
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation in the project

Enforcement will always aim to be fair, consistent, and based on objective behavior.

---

## Philosophy

BitGenesis is not just a software project.

It is an attempt to design a structured cognitive architecture with clarity, modularity, and long-term evolution in mind.

Respect for the system and its design principles is considered essential for meaningful contribution.

---

## Contact

For any concerns related to this Code of Conduct, please open an issue in the repository.
